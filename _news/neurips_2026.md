[Explanation Multiplicity in SHAP: Characterization and Assessment](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.12654) was accepted to the [NeurIPS 2026](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026) Datasets & Benchmarks track. Much of the credit here goes to Daniel (Hyunseung) Hwang, who led the project, alongside Seungeun Lee. This work shows that SHAP explanations can differ substantially across repeated runs even with the model, input, and prediction held fixed. Thus, a single-run SHAP output is best interpreted as a draw from a distribution over possible outputs, not as an some authoritative artifact.
